Estimate only. Consumption and upkeep figures are class averages — driving style, load, terrain and weather shift the result by roughly ±25%.

Upkeep is the full cost of owning the car, spread over the distance driven: wear & service (inspections, repairs, tyres, brakes), insurance & tax, and depreciation — the largest block of all. Figures are class averages in cents per kilometre on the ADAC basis of a new car held 5 years at 15,000 km a year, derived from published per-month cost splits.
